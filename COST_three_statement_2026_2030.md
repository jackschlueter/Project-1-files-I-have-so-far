## Challenge to my assumption

**Draft attack for the partner to ask:** “Your 7% membership-fee growth assumes no further fee increase, but the FY2025 filing says the fee increase produced about 40% of that year's membership-income growth. What paid-member, renewal, and Executive-upgrade path supplies the remaining 7% each year, and what reported result would make you lower it?”

**My answer (two sentences):** I used 7% because FY2025 fee revenue grew 10.3% and roughly 40% of its dollar increase came from the fee increase, leaving about 6.2% growth from other drivers; my forecast asks for a modest improvement from those drivers. I would lower it if reported fee growth without a new price increase stays below 7% or renewal and Executive-upgrade trends weaken, and raise it only if sustained filings show stronger member growth.

Source for the fee increase contribution and membership results: [Costco FY2025 10-K, Item 7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm).
