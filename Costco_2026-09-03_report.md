# Costco Wholesale Corporation — Company Research Report

## Company and valuation date

Costco Wholesale Corporation operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites under the ticker COST. [Costco FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, p. 3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

This report uses the August 31, 2026 market close, when COST closed at $943.89, with a $418.60 billion equity value and a $406.83 billion enterprise value. [StockAnalysis market data, August 31, 2026](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cost/market-cap/)

## What management said and what happened

In its FY2023 Form 10-K, management intended to spend approximately $4.4–$4.6 billion on fiscal 2024 capital expenditures and to open up to 28 warehouses, including one relocation. [Costco FY2023 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983223000042/cost-20230903.htm)

Costco spent $4.710 billion on fiscal 2024 capital expenditures and opened 30 warehouses, including one relocation. [Costco FY2024 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983224000049/cost-20240901.htm)

Management also stated that it expected to increase private-label penetration, but its later filings do not disclose a penetration percentage that can confirm or reject that objective. [Costco FY2023 Form 10-K, Item 1, p. 7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983223000042/cost-20230903.htm) [Costco FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, p. 7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

Costco’s warehouse count increased from 861 at the end of fiscal 2023 to 914 at the end of fiscal 2025. [Costco FY2023 Form 10-K, Item 1, p. 3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983223000042/cost-20230903.htm) [Costco FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, p. 3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

## Operating performance

Fiscal 2025 revenue was $275.235 billion, operating income was $10.383 billion, and diluted EPS was $18.21. [Costco FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, p. 34](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

Cash and cash equivalents were $14.161 billion at fiscal 2025 year-end, compared with $5.788 billion of current and noncurrent long-term debt. [Costco FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, p. 35](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

Fiscal 2025 adjusted comparable sales increased 7.6%, adjusted e-commerce comparable sales increased 16.1%, and membership-fee revenue reached $5.323 billion. [Costco FY2025 earnings release, September 25, 2025](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2025/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Operating-Results/default.aspx)

In fiscal Q3 2026, net sales increased 11.6% to $69.154 billion, adjusted comparable sales increased 6.6%, and membership fees were $1.373 billion. [Costco FY2026 Q3 earnings release, May 28, 2026](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-and-Year-To-Date-Operating-Results-For-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx)

At May 10, 2026, Costco reported $18.946 billion of cash and cash equivalents, $5.670 billion of long-term debt excluding the current portion, and 928 warehouses worldwide. [Costco FY2026 Q3 Form 10-Q, Item 1, p. 5; Item 2, p. 20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000051/cost-20260510.htm)

## Management’s current priorities and trade-offs

On the May 2026 earnings call, management said it expected 26 net warehouse openings in fiscal 2026, with two locations delayed into fiscal 2027, while continuing to target more than 30 annual net openings in the longer run. [Costco FY2026 Q3 earnings call transcript, May 28, 2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/)

Management reported that digitally enabled comparable sales grew 21.5%, paid members reached 82.9 million, paid Executive memberships reached 41.2 million, and the U.S./Canada renewal rate was 92.2% in fiscal Q3 2026. [Costco FY2026 Q3 earnings call transcript, May 28, 2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/)

Management also reported that gross margin declined 21 basis points in the quarter and described its price reductions as strategic investments in member value. [Costco FY2026 Q3 earnings call transcript, May 28, 2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/)

In a May 2026 Economic Club of Chicago interview, CEO Ron Vachris described AI as a tool to support employees rather than replace them and said merchandise-selection decisions would remain with buyers. [Fortune, May 28, 2026, reporting on the Economic Club interview](https://fortune.com/2026/05/28/costco-ceo-ron-vachris-artifical-intelligence-not-stealing-jobs-tech-improving-work-retail-giant-success/)

## Questions that remain

Can Costco sustain comparable-sales growth, membership growth, and warehouse expansion while protecting margins as it invests in lower prices, digital capabilities, and new locations?

Does the August 31, 2026 enterprise value of $406.83 billion leave enough upside after using realistic assumptions for sales growth, margins, capital spending, and valuation-multiple compression? [StockAnalysis market data, August 31, 2026](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cost/market-cap/)

What evidence over the next two quarters would show that Costco’s membership model is no longer converting warehouse expansion and higher member fees into sustainable per-share free-cash-flow growth?

## Call

**Watch / defer — do not initiate yet.** Costco’s operating execution, member metrics, and expansion progress are strong, but the current valuation and ongoing margin trade-off require a scenario-tested expected-return case before an investment decision. [Costco FY2026 Q3 earnings call transcript, May 28, 2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/) [StockAnalysis market data, August 31, 2026](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cost/market-cap/)

This call would change to initiate if a scenario-tested valuation shows sufficient expected return and margin of safety after realistic downside assumptions for comparable sales, margins, capital spending, and the exit multiple.
